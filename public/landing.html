Common questions

Is Meridian a therapist or a lawyer?

No. Meridian helps you organize information and think through next steps; it isn’t a substitute for professional advice or crisis support.

What does Meridian actually do?

Keep important documents, schedules, and dates together in one private space. Optional AI tools can help you understand a document, think through a situation, or prepare a message.

Is my data private?

Your account is not shared with your co-parent. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. When you use AI features, messages and relevant document text are sent to Anthropic to generate responses. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is it really free?

Standard signups get a 3-day free trial. Eligible professional referrals get 14 days. Then $4.99/month. Cancel anytime.

Can my ex see what I write here?

Your co-parent does not have shared account access or a co-parent portal. AI features send messages and relevant document text to Anthropic to generate responses. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why Meridian exists

I went through a divorce. It sucks. It's not easy, and no one wins.

Many nights I didn't know what to do, who to call, or if I was going to be okay. I couldn't afford to ask a lawyer every question. I didn't want pity. I just wanted someone to help me think clearly when I couldn't.

That's why I built Meridian.
