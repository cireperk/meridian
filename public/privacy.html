Privacy Policy

Effective January 2026

Meridian is built for people navigating one of the hardest chapters of their lives. We believe your privacy isn't just a feature — it's a right. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how your information is handled.

What we collect

  • Account info: Your name and email address, used solely for authentication and to personalize your experience.
  • Conversations: Messages you exchange with Meridian's assistant are stored securely so you can access your history across sessions.
  • Documents: Files you upload to your Vault (decrees, agreements, etc.) are stored in encrypted cloud storage tied to your account.
  • Calendar events: Dates, times, and notes you add to your co-parenting calendar.
  • Referral attribution: If you sign up with a professional referral link, we store its practice attribution with your account to apply the extended trial.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data. We share information with service providers that help operate Meridian, including Anthropic when you use AI features.
  • We never show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • Your co-parent and other users do not have shared access to your account or data.

How we protect your data

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS and encrypted at rest.
  • Authentication is handled with industry-standard security practices including bcrypt password hashing and secure token management.
  • Document storage uses access-controlled cloud storage tied to your account.

AI conversations

When you use Meridian's AI features, your message and relevant document text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. Meridian stores conversation history and documents in your account so you can use them in the app. Anthropic processes the information it receives under its applicable policies and terms. Do not submit information you are not comfortable having processed by these services.

Product analytics

Meridian records a limited set of first-party event names, such as signup steps, guide searches, and professional referral requests, to understand how the service is used. Event records do not include message or document content, search terms, email addresses, or account identifiers. Events are processed in our hosting provider's logs.

Google and Apple sign-in

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only your name and email address. We do not access your contacts, calendar, drive, or any other services.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual conversations, documents, and calendar events in the app. To request deletion of your entire account and associated data, contact privacy@mymeridianapp.com.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update this policy and notify you in the app where appropriate.

Questions? Reach us at support@mymeridianapp.com
